A working demo of the app. Every figure below is editable, and the line redraws as you type. Nothing you type leaves this page: there is no server behind it and nothing is stored, so a reload starts you over with the example.

The app itself runs on your iPhone, keeps everything there, and is exact to the cent. This demo is the same arithmetic in a browser, close enough to show you what it does.
