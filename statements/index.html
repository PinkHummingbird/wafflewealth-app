Read a bank statement, here on your device.

Add the PDF your bank sends you, or a Wise CSV. This page finds each account in it, the balance on the statement’s last day and every line, then checks that the lines add up to the statement’s own totals.

The PDF is read in this browser tab and goes nowhere. The page is not allowed to connect to anything, and nothing is saved: a reload or Clear forgets it.

    Read in this tab

    The PDF reader (PDF.js, from Mozilla) is served by this site and runs in your browser. Your file is opened from your device and never uploaded.

    No way out

    The page’s security policy sets connect-src 'none': the browser blocks any request it tries to make. It has no visit counter either, unlike the rest of this site.

    Nothing kept

    No cookies and no browser storage. Close or reload the tab and every statement is gone.
