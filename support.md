# WaffleWealth — Support

Questions, bug reports, or feedback: **wafflewealth.app.support@gmail.com**

WaffleWealth is a net worth projection app for iPhone. You tell it what you have, what you earn and what you spend, and it draws your net worth forward month by month. It is not a transaction tracker: there are no bank connections, no receipts, and no account to create.

Everything is computed on your device. There is no account, no server, and no data collection.
