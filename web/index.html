Your iCloud, not ours

Everything goes to the private part of your own iCloud, which only you can read. WaffleWealth has no server and no copy, and its developer can't see your iCloud.

Read on your own device

A statement is read here in your browser, or by WaffleWealth on your iPhone, never on a server. Every figure is checked against the statement's own totals before anything is added.

Nothing extra on this page

No visit counter, no tracking. It loads Apple's own iCloud library and the site's fonts, and nothing else.
