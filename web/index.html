For WaffleWealth on iPhone

Your statements, from your computer to your phone.

Upload the statements your banks send you, and type in money in and out, here on a big screen. They go into your own iCloud, and WaffleWealth on your iPhone picks them up the next time it opens.

  1. On your iPhone, switch the copy on.In WaffleWealth, open Settings and turn on “Keep a copy in iCloud”.
  2. Here, sign in with the same Apple ID.Apple signs you in. We never see your password, and there's no account to make.
  3. Drop a statement in.On your phone it's under Activity, ready to read and add to your money.

Your iCloud, not ours

Everything goes to the private part of your own iCloud, which only you can read. WaffleWealth has no server and no copy, and its developer can't see your iCloud.

Read on your phone

Statements are read by WaffleWealth on your iPhone, which checks every figure against the statement's own totals before anything is added.

Nothing extra on this page

No visit counter, no tracking. It loads Apple's own iCloud library and the site's fonts, and nothing else.
