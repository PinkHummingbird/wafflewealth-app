How it handles money.

Four rules the engine keeps, whatever you enter. They are why the line can be trusted.

Currencies

Balances keep their currency.

Held in GBP, DKK or anything else on the list, and translated only when shown — so you read it in the currency you think in.

History

Updates keep the old balance.

Every update is a new entry, never an edit. Your history stays intact, so you can see how old projections held up.

Growth

Savings earn nothing unless you say so.

Cash accumulates as cash. Returns apply only to accounts you mark invested, and interest pays out as cash unless you say otherwise.

Honesty

A falling line is drawn falling.

Spend more than you earn and the line goes down. Never clamped at zero, never smoothed, never softened.

Nothing leaves your phone.

No account, no sign-up. Balances, account names and projections live only on your iPhone, encrypted while it is locked.

  • No trackingNo analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs. The app has no server of its own.
  • One optional requestDaily exchange rates, off until you turn it on. It sends a three-letter currency code — never a balance, amount, account name or category.
  • Yours to takeExport everything, any time, including an encrypted backup only your passphrase opens.
  • Yours to deleteDelete the app and it is all gone. The database lives inside it.
