How the Coast FIRE number is worked out

FIRE number = yearly spending × 100 ÷ withdrawal rate. At 4%, that's 25 times what you spend in a year.

Coast FIRE number = FIRE number ÷ (1 + return)years to retirement. It's what you'd need invested today for growth alone to reach your FIRE number on time. It falls every year you're younger than your retirement age, so the line it draws slopes up towards your FIRE number.

Once your invested money is above that line, saving more brings retirement earlier but isn't needed to retire on time. This page works out the arithmetic; it doesn't tell you what to do.
